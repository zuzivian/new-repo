# HQ v2: information architecture

**Problem (Nat, 25 Nov):** HQ is boring and cluttered, and the key information is buried. It should show the right thing at the right time.

**Principle:** HQ is about Nat's life, not the machine. Every screen should pass a 3-second test. At a glance he should know three things:

1. the one thing to do next;
2. whether anything needs him;
3. whether he's on track (capacity at or under 70%, and his fall goals).

## Layout (mobile first, top to bottom)

1. **Greeting and summary line.** The greeting matches the moment ("Good morning", "Heads up" before a meeting, "Winding down"). Below it, one line of numbers: needs and % booked.
2. **Red banner, shown only when something is broken.** That means data more than 3 h old, stuck or failed agent runs, a critical fleet issue, or a stalled agent. Otherwise nothing is shown.
3. **The Now card.** Exactly one, chosen by context. Checks run top to bottom, and the first match wins.
   | When (PT) | Now card shows |
   |---|---|
   | A meeting starts within 45 min (or started under 30 min ago) | The prep brief: title, place, the person and "last time", the first 3 questions, and a link to the full brief |
   | Sat, Sun or Mon morning, with a week-plan need open | The week plan, answerable in place |
   | 18:00–04:00 | Today, done: fleet tasks finished, decisions made, coffee chats this week, and tomorrow's first thing |
   | 04:00–10:00 | Today: the first thing to do, plus meetings today, needs, and % of today booked |
   | 10:00–18:00 | The next decision, answerable in one tap; "All clear ✨" if nothing is waiting |
4. **Strip of 3 chips:** needs (with how many are due), capacity %, and coffee chats out of 40. Each chip links down the page.
5. **Needs you.** One queue that merges `needs` and Nat's live `nat_actions` (proposed only). It's deduplicated by title and sorted by tier, then due date. Each card shows the title, due date, estimated minutes and a 3-line detail with More, plus one-tap answers (★ = recommended). Answering plays a check animation and the card slides out. When the queue empties it shows "Inbox zero 🎉". Items in the "later" tier are folded into one line.
6. **On track?** A capacity gauge with a 70% tick and the 7-day bars, a ring for coffee chats out of 40 with the countdown, a ring for warm companies out of 20, and the applications funnel (tracked, applied, interviewing, offers).
7. **Fleet & machine →** the classic page (`index.html#fleet`), which still holds agents, usage, cost, burn, projects and issues. These are hidden from the default view.

Decisions the fleet made are an FYI, not a need. They only appear as the evening count.

## Rules kept from classic HQ
- Real numbers only; a missing value shows "—", never 0.
- No names or personal data in the source; everything comes from `/api/data` at runtime.
- No new libraries, CDNs, backend, or `vercel.json` changes.
- Data comes from `/api/data`, falling back to `data.json`. Answers go to `/api/answer`, falling back to a prefilled GitHub issue.
- `?at=<ISO time>` simulates a moment, for testing and screenshots.

## Data wanted (Fleet Ops)
- **`meetings[]` is empty today.** The meeting Now card needs `{id, when, title, location, people[{name, who, last_time}], questions[]}` for the next 72 h. This matters most.
- **Week reflection:** a `week_review` block on Sun/Mon (done vs planned, wins), so the weekend card is more than accept/adjust.
- **Wins:** a `wins[]` list for the last 24 h (e.g. "PR merged", "coffee chat logged", "application sent"), so the evening card can celebrate specifics.
- **Streak:** days in a row with the queue at zero, or with the plan kept, so the inbox-zero moment can say "3-day streak".
